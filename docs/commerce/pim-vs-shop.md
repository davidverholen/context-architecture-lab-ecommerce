# PIM vs Shop

Principle: PIM optimizes product data governance and enrichment; Shopify optimizes customer experience and conversion.

PIM/Akeneo owns:

- Product family and enrichment workflow.
- Attribute completeness and quality.
- Canonical product vocabulary.
- Governance before projection.

Shopify owns:

- Customer-facing product presentation.
- Commerce status, merchandising, pricing display, and sales channel behavior.
- Native product fields, variants, metafields, and metaobjects as projection tools.

Do not treat Shopify as the product master in this lab.
